1. INTRODUCTION

Our commitment to your privacy.

TopLend Financing and Lending respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store and share personal information when you visit our website, apply for a loan, use our services or contact us.

We handle personal information in accordance with Botswana's Data Protection Act No. 18 of 2024 and other applicable laws.

2. PERSONAL INFORMATION WE COLLECT

Personal Information We Collect

Depending on your interaction with TopLend, we may collect:

  • Identity information: name, national identity or passport number, date of birth and gender.
  • Contact information: telephone number, email address and physical address.
  • Financial and loan information: bank account details, bank statements, income, employment details, loan applications, account information and repayment history.
  • Technical information: IP address, browser and device details, and website usage information.
  • Other information: information provided in communications, enquiries or complaints.
3. HOW WE COLLECT PERSONAL INFORMATION

How We Collect Personal Information

We may collect information directly from you through loan applications, forms, enquiries and communications with TopLend or our authorised Sales Agents.

This may include supporting documents such as identification documents, payslips, bank statements and proof of payment, as well as information provided when requesting settlement quotations or discussing loans, repayments, collections or complaints.

Where lawful and necessary, we may receive information from third parties such as employers, banks and credit bureaus. Our website may also collect technical information through server logs and cookies. The information collected depends on the service and circumstances.

4. HOW WE USE PERSONAL INFORMATION

How We Use Personal Information

We may use personal information to:

  • Assess loan applications and verify information supplied to us.
  • Administer loan accounts, repayments and settlements.
  • Respond to enquiries and communicate with customers.
  • Meet legal, regulatory, reporting and record-keeping requirements.
  • Prevent, detect and investigate fraud or other unlawful activity.
  • Protect our website, systems, customers and business operations.
  • Establish, exercise or defend legal claims.
5. WHY WE PROCESS PERSONAL INFORMATION

Why We Process Personal Information

We process personal information where permitted by law, including where necessary to provide services or fulfil contractual obligations, comply with legal obligations, pursue legitimate business interests, or where you have given consent and consent is required.

6. SHARING PERSONAL INFORMATION

Sharing Personal Information

Where necessary and lawful, we may share personal information with banks, employers, credit bureaus and other relevant parties; technology and service providers; regulators, law enforcement agencies, courts and other authorities; and parties involved in debt recovery or enforcement of our rights.

We may also share information with other parties where you authorise us to do so or where the law permits or requires it. We take appropriate steps to ensure that service providers handle personal information responsibly.

7. PROTECTION OF PERSONAL INFORMATION

Protection of Personal Information

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, alteration and disclosure.

No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure. Please contact us immediately if you believe your personal information has been compromised.

8. HOW LONG WE KEEP INFORMATION

How Long We Keep Information

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, including to meet legal, regulatory, contractual and record-keeping obligations. Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and applicable requirements.

9. COOKIES AND WEBSITE TECHNOLOGIES

Cookies and Website Technologies

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to support website functionality and understand how visitors use the site. You may manage cookies through your browser settings or any cookie preference tool provided on the website.

10. TRANSFERS OUTSIDE BOTSWANA

Transfers Outside Botswana

Some service providers may store or process personal information outside Botswana. Where this occurs, we take steps to protect personal information, including transfers to countries recognised as providing adequate protection by the Information and Data Protection Commission, the use of appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the Commission, or obtaining explicit consent where required.

11. YOUR RIGHTS

Your Rights

Subject to applicable legal requirements and limitations, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to personal information held about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request erasure, subject to legal and contractual obligations.
  • Request restriction of certain processing activities.
  • Object to processing, including direct marketing or other applicable purposes.
  • Request data portability where applicable.

To exercise these rights, please contact us using the details in Section 13. We may need to verify your identity before responding to a request.

12. THIRD-PARTY WEBSITES

Third-Party Websites

Our website may contain links to third-party websites. TopLend does not control the privacy practices of those websites. We encourage you to review their privacy notices before providing personal information.
