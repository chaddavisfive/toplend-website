WHO WE ARE

Helping bridge financial gaps with confidence.

Water Solutions Mining Botswana T/A TopLend Financing & Lending (PTY) Ltd is a 100% citizen-owned financial institution established in 2017.

The company began operations with a focused offering of Purchase Order Financing and Pawn Shop Services. Driven by market demand and consistent growth, TopLend has expanded its portfolio to include short- and long-term personal lending solutions.

Headquartered in Gaborone CBD, with a growing national footprint, TopLend is committed to delivering tailor-made financial solutions that empower individuals and businesses alike.
